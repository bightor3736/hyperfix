# Hyperfix — Content Kickoff (Just Start positioning)

Ready-to-film TikTok/Reels plan, aligned to the painkiller pivot: **helping
ADHD brains start the task they're avoiding.** The product now matches this
message (Just Start flow + "you started" win + forgiving streak + share card),
so it's time to open the tap.

The rule that makes this work: **the video is about your brain, not the app.**
The app appears for ~1.5s while you name a task and start it. Curiosity beats
pitch every time with this audience.

---

## The 3 content pillars

1. **Relatable pain** — "this is so me" task-paralysis content. Highest reach.
2. **The trick** — show the mechanic (the 5-minute lie) actually working on you.
3. **The win/flex** — the "I finally started X" + share-card moment.

Post 1×/day for 30 days across all three; double down on whatever hits.

---

## 20 ready-to-film hooks

**Pillar 1 — Relatable pain (lead with these — widest reach)**
1. "POV: you've been staring at the same task for 3 days and it's not even hard."
2. "Things that take 5 minutes that I've been avoiding for 3 weeks: [list]."
3. "Explaining task paralysis to people who just say 'why don't you just do it'."
4. "My brain when there's a tiny boring task: *system overload*."
5. "The task is small. The wall in front of it is enormous. IYKYK."
6. "Rating the tasks I'm avoiding by how stupidly easy they actually are."
7. "It's not laziness. I literally cannot make my body start. Here's what that feels like."

**Pillar 2 — The trick (show the mechanic)**
8. "How I trick my ADHD brain into starting things I've avoided for weeks."
9. "The 5-minute lie that actually gets me to start (you can quit after, you won't)."
10. "I stopped trying to 'finish' and only let myself 'start' — everything changed."
11. "The smallest-first-step hack: I don't write the essay, I type the title."
12. "Body double yourself: name it, set 5 min, go. Watch me do the thing I hate."
13. "Why 'just do 2 minutes' works on ADHD brains (the dopamine reason)."
14. "I gave myself XP for *starting* instead of finishing and my brain finally cooperated."

**Pillar 3 — The win / flex (drives the share loop)**
15. "Day 1 vs day 14 of only-have-to-start mode. I have object permanence now."
16. "I finally started [the thing I avoided for a month]. Here's the card I got."
17. "My 'started' streak hit 30 days. I've never kept a streak in my life."
18. "Reply to @comment: yes there's an app that rewards you for STARTING not finishing."
19. "The forgiving streak that doesn't punish me for a bad week (finally)."
20. "Showing my 'still waiting on you' list — the tasks that haunt me, gamified."

## Format checklist (per video)

- [ ] Text hook on screen in the first 1.5s, and you're already talking.
- [ ] Founder face, vertical, shot on phone — no "ad" energy.
- [ ] App on screen ~1.5s max, mid-story (naming + starting a task).
- [ ] Trending sound underneath your talking where possible.
- [ ] One clear feeling per video (seen, relieved, or proud).
- [ ] Reply to every comment in the first hour (engagement window).
- [ ] Repurpose every post to IG Reels + YouTube Shorts same day.

---

## 30-day cadence

- **Week 1:** 7 posts, mostly Pillar 1 (find your relatable voice). Lurk + comment daily on r/ADHD, r/adhdwomen.
- **Week 2:** Mix in Pillar 2. Start the creator seeding list (see GROWTH.md §7). First reply-to-comment video.
- **Week 3:** Add Pillar 3 once you have real streak/share screenshots. Send first 15 creator DMs.
- **Week 4:** Review analytics — keep the 2-3 hook formats that hit, drop the rest. Plan to repeat winners.

---

## What to measure

- **Watch-through %** (first 3s especially) — the single best signal of a working hook.
- **Saves + shares** > likes — saves mean "this is me / I need this."
- **Comment "what app is this"** — the buy signal; answer with a reply video, not a hard link.
- **Profile → site clicks → installs** — track with a single bio link + UTM.

One line: *show up daily with your own face, tell the truth about not being able
to start, and let the "you started" moment do the selling.*
